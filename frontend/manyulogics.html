◈ WHO WE ARE

Built in India.
For India.

Manyu Logics was founded with a single conviction — that India's most critical institutions deserve AI infrastructure built for their context, not adapted from tools designed for Silicon Valley.

Global AI platforms were not designed for Indian compliance frameworks, classified environments, or the linguistic and document diversity of our healthcare and financial systems. We build what didn't exist.

Our work sits at the intersection of sovereign deployment, regulatory compliance and practical AI orchestration. Every product we ship runs entirely within our client's infrastructure — no external API calls, no foreign cloud dependency, no recurring per-query costs.

We are a Bengaluru-based MSME, registration in progress. Our first product, MCPilot, is live and serving Defence, BFSI and Healthcare verticals.
